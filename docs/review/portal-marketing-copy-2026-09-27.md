## Preview opening rule
- Frame 1 must match the static cover.
- Move into real gameplay immediately after the cover.
- Avoid logo-only fades, black frames, promotional text, social/app icons, and mouse cursor.
- Keep preview silent and under 20 seconds.

## Screenshot selection
Use actual runtime screenshots only. Prefer:
1. one clear core-play screen,
2. one progression/choice screen,
3. one result/boss/chronicle payoff screen.

Do not use isolated generated asset sheets as store screenshots.
