# Six-game retention phase — 2026-09-27

## Goal
The visual-fidelity round is complete. The next phase improves repeat play without changing the identity or core loop of any title.

## Cross-game rules
- Do not replace the existing core mechanic.
- Add one meaningful decision or one medium-term goal at a time.
- Every new system needs a visible reason to care now and a reason to return later.
- Prefer code-native progression over new art unless the screen actually needs another asset.
- Mobile readability and one-tap retry remain release gates.

## Title priorities

### 三国ポチポチ
- Deepen expedition route choice rather than adding a new combat system.
- Make risk/reward legible before committing to a route.
- Strengthen general/team identity through modifiers already supported by the expedition loop.
- Done: two viable route decisions can produce visibly different rewards or risk profiles.

### Potion Workshop
- Use town demand, orders and prestige as the medium-term loop.
- Surface the next useful upgrade and the next town objective clearly.
- Avoid adding passive decorative systems that do not affect decisions.
- Done: a player can identify the next 2–5 minute objective without opening a help screen.

### Karma Quest
- Increase replay value through clearly different 12-year outcomes rather than more screens.
- Make prior choices visible in the final chronicle and next-run motivation.
- Keep outcomes deferred-loaded.
- Done: two intentionally different choice patterns produce recognizably different final summaries.

### 剣戟の森
- Build replay value through weapon/style/enemy-wave composition and boss patterns.
- Avoid adding another action button unless it creates a new decision.
- Keep ATK / SKL / GRD as the primary mobile hierarchy.
- Done: two loadouts/styles feel meaningfully different across early waves and a boss encounter.

### 覇拳伝
- Deepen stance reading, team composition and gauntlet/story value.
- Preserve the current rock-paper-scissors combat core.
- Results should immediately suggest a reason to retry: rival read, team switch, next chapter or streak.
- Done: result flow makes the next decision obvious and team choice changes at least one tactical preference.

### Color Match
- Improve the difficulty curve, streak/fever payoff and result analysis.
- Never turn it into match-3, RPG or combat.
- Done: early rounds teach the rule, mid rounds pressure attention, final results explain performance and invite another run.

## Shared retention checklist
- [ ] First meaningful choice within 30 seconds.
- [ ] Clear next objective after every result screen.
- [ ] Retry in one obvious tap/click.
- [ ] No dead-end result screen.
- [ ] Progress survives reload when the title has persistent progression.
- [ ] A 5–10 minute session exposes at least one new decision, unlock, difficulty shift or objective.
- [ ] No system exists only to add visual clutter.

## PR rule
One PR should satisfy one complete player-facing outcome, not a list of disconnected micro-polishes.
